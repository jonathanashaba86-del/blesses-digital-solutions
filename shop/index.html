How it works

How an order runs

You get a text at every stage. If something is out of stock the picker calls you before substituting anything.

STEP 01

You order

Fill the basket, choose your zone, pay by mobile money, card, or cash at the door.

STEP 02

We pick

A picker walks the aisles with your list. Weighed lines go on the scale and the price is set there.

STEP 03

We confirm

You get the final weights and total by SMS. Approve, or swap anything you would rather not take.

STEP 04

The rider delivers

Cold lines travel in a chilled box. The rider calls when he reaches your landmark.

Delivery

Where we deliver

Pick your zone to set the fee and the minimum basket. Free delivery on anything over the threshold, everywhere we go.

Order before the cut-off

Anything placed before 2:00 PM goes out in the same afternoon run. After that we pick first thing the next morning.
