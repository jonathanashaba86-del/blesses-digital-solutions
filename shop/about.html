A real shop. With a delivery arm.

Not a warehouse and not a marketplace. What you order online is picked off the same shelves our walk-in customers buy from, at the same prices, by the same staff.

How we run

We opened on Kira Road with four staff and a single chiller. The shop grew the way shops do — by being where people already were, and by not messing anyone about on price.

Delivery started because customers kept asking. Rather than hand it to an app that would take a cut and mark the prices up, we built it ourselves. That is the only reason the shelf price and the online price are the same number.

Weighed lines, honestly

Meat, fish and loose produce cannot be priced exactly before they are weighed. Most shops round up and hope. We charge you the estimate, weigh the line at the counter, text you the real figure, and settle the difference either way. If the scale reads under, you get money back.

Substitutions

If a line is finished the picker rings you before putting anything else in the bag. You choose the alternative, or drop it and get refunded. Nobody decides on your behalf.

Receipts

Every order carries a URA-verified fiscal receipt with a QR code. It travels with your shopping and a copy sits in your account.
