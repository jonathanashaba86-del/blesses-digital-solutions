EmployCore

White-Label AI Workforce

“The same AI team running BDS, now working for your business.”

The problem

Why this exists.

Most SMEs cannot afford a receptionist, a bookkeeper and a marketer, so the owner does all three badly, at midnight, instead of running the business.

The experience

How it should feel.

EmployCore should be onboarded like a real hire, never switched on like software. The client “meets” their new AI team by name, with a short introduction and a one-page employee handbook describing exactly what each one does and does not do. Every week, the owner receives a one-page manager’s report — the same weekly rhythm BDS already runs on itself — summarising what got done, what’s outstanding, and what needs a decision. The rarest, most premium detail: the client can watch their AI team’s activity log in real time, the same transparency BDS uses internally, which is the one thing no generic chatbot vendor can show.

Specification

Core capabilities & premium signature touches.

The left column is what the product does. The right column is why it is worth paying a premium for — the details a competitor will not bother to build.

Core capabilities

  • Modular roles the client mixes: Reception, Bookkeeping, Marketing, Sales follow-up
  • Reception role built on the same voice/WhatsApp layer as VoiceCore
  • Bookkeeping role wired into InvCore for invoices and expense tracking
  • Weekly one-page manager’s report to the owner
  • Read-only by default on anything sensitive — nothing acts without approval until switched on

Premium signature touches

  • A named, branded persona per hired role, introduced like a real new employee
  • A printed or PDF employee handbook for each role
  • A monthly review call styled like a genuine staff performance review

Build notes

What it extends.

Productises BDS’s own internal Veronica / Jarvis / Marvin / Mannie operating model as a permissioned, multi-tenant service — reusing the Reception layer from VoiceCore and the Bookkeeping layer from InvCore.

Position & pricing

How EmployCore is sold.

Priced per role hired, like fractional staff rather than software seats — the full team sold as a bundle at a premium to any single role.
