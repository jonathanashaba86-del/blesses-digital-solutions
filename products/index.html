Release order

  • Line 01 / 04

    VoiceCore — AI Reception & Call Intelligence

    Sold as “the cost of one missed customer a month,” not a software licence — setup deposit plus a monthly seat fee tiered by call volume.

    ScoreCore — AI Credit Intelligence for SACCOs
