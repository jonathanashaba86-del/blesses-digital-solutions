ScoreCore

AI Credit Intelligence for SACCOs

“Turn every mobile-money transaction into a lending decision a board can defend.”
MinutesFrom upload to credit memo
1 pagePlain-language memo
FullAudit trail for UMRA

The problem

Why this exists.

Good borrowers are turned away daily for having no bank statement — only a phone full of MTN MoMo and Airtel Money history. Manual loan review takes SACCOs days, and the final call is often a guess dressed up as policy.

The experience

How it should feel.

ScoreCore should never feel like a black box. A loan officer connects or uploads a member’s mobile-money history and, within minutes, receives a clean, single-page credit memo — the kind a commercial bank underwriter would produce — written in plain language: reasons to lend, reasons to be cautious, and a confidence band, never a bare number. It is watermarked with the SACCO’s own logo, so a small rural SACCO can hand a member, a donor, or a regulator something that reads like it came from a modern financial institution.

Specification

Core capabilities & premium signature touches.

The left column is what the product does. The right column is why it is worth paying a premium for — the details a competitor will not bother to build.

Core capabilities

  • Mobile-money transaction pattern analysis (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money)
  • Scoring model tuned to Ugandan financial behaviour — e.g. regular family transfers read as stability, not suspicion
  • Plain-language, explainable credit memos, not a bare score
  • Repayment tracking integration with PayCore UG
  • Fraud and score-gaming pattern flags
  • Full audit trail for UMRA and internal compliance review

Premium signature touches

  • Branded, watermarked one-page credit memo per loan applicant
  • A boardroom-style portfolio risk dashboard for SACCO leadership
  • Quarterly “Portfolio Health” report, designed like an annual-report page, fit for an AGM or a donor meeting

Rollout

From signature to live, in four moves.

Compliance framing

Data-handling, consent and retention set out under the ComplyCore UG framework before a single statement is read.

Data connection

Loan officers connect or upload MTN MoMo and Airtel Money history for a member.

Model calibration

Scoring bands tuned to the SACCO’s own book and to Ugandan financial behaviour.

Memo & portfolio rollout

Watermarked credit memos in production, leadership dashboard live, first quarterly Portfolio Health report issued.

Ideal client

SACCOsVillage banks & VSLAsMicrofinance institutionsEmployer loan schemesDonor-funded lending programmes

Build notes

What it extends.

Sits beside ComplyCore UG — SACCOs are already ComplyCore’s target client — and reports into PayCore UG for repayment tracking. ComplyCore’s compliance framing is what earns the trust to handle this data at all.

Position & pricing

How ScoreCore is sold.

Per-SACCO licence plus a per-score usage fee, positioned against the cost of one bad loan avoided — not against the price of software.

  • Annual per-SACCO licence
  • Per-score usage fee

Exact figures are quoted after a briefing call — they depend on volume and scope. Founding-client terms apply while this line is in build.
