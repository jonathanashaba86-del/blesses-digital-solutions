RadarCore

AI Visibility Intelligence

“Google isn’t where your next customer is asking anymore.”
3+AI engines audited
1 numberTracked weekly: were we mentioned
Head to headClient vs three rivals

The problem

Why this exists.

Clients whose websites BDS has already built have never once checked whether ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity mention their business when someone asks “best clinic in Entebbe” or “best hotel near Lake Victoria.” Right now, most are invisible.

The experience

How it should feel.

RadarCore should feel like a boardroom scorecard, not an SEO report. The client receives a magazine-styled “AI Visibility Report”: the real questions a customer might ask an AI, whether the business was mentioned, which competitors were named instead, and exactly why — missing structured data, thin reviews, unclear service pages. A small live dashboard then tracks one number over time — were we mentioned this week — checked the way an owner checks a stock price. The most premium page in the report: the client versus their three biggest rivals, visibility measured head to head.

Specification

Core capabilities & premium signature touches.

The left column is what the product does. The right column is why it is worth paying a premium for — the details a competitor will not bother to build.

Core capabilities

  • Multi-engine AI-mention auditing (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and others)
  • Competitor AI-visibility comparison, head to head
  • Structured data and schema implementation on the client’s site
  • “Answer-ready” content rewrites engineered to be cited
  • Monthly re-audit with a running visibility trend

Premium signature touches

  • Magazine-styled AI Visibility Report, not a spreadsheet
  • A live “AI Mention Tracker” dashboard the client checks monthly
  • An “AI-Visibility Verified” badge for the client’s own site — a trust signal that quietly markets BDS too

Rollout

From signature to live, in four moves.

Question set

We write the real questions a customer would ask an AI about this category, in this town.

Multi-engine audit

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and others queried; mentions, omissions and named rivals recorded.

Fix the causes

Structured data, schema and answer-ready content rewrites shipped to the client’s site.

Track & re-audit

Live AI Mention Tracker plus a monthly re-audit and running visibility trend.

Build notes

What it extends.

The audit draws on the AI-citation tooling already connected to BDS’s workflow; content rewrites plug into the existing web-build service; the dashboard reuses the portfolio hub’s Netlify/Supabase stack.

Position & pricing

How RadarCore is sold.

Sold as a premium retainer add-on to every new website build — “your site is finished, now let’s make sure the AI is answering with your name in it” — never as a stand-alone discount service.
