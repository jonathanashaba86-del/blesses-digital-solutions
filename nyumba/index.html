Ntinda workshop

Why four weeks,
and not four hours

Anyone can hand you a sofa this afternoon. It will be stapled softwood over foam that has already given up. Ours takes four weeks because the frame is mortised and glued, the seat is webbed rather than boarded, and the fabric is cut to the piece you actually chose.

Spec & price

Measure, draw, price. Quotation with volume breaks and a price hold.

Day 1–3

Cut & joint

Kiln-dried stock cut to the drawing. Frames mortised, glued and clamped overnight.

Week 1–2

Upholster & finish

Webbing, HR35 foam, your fabric. Hand-rubbed oil or lacquer on timber.

Week 3

Deliver & sign off

Our truck, our crew, our snag list. Nothing invoiced as complete until you say so.
