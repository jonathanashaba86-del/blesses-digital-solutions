How we work

Four steps. You always know where the project is.

No disappearing for three weeks. Every project has one point of contact and a fixed date you can hold us to.

01 / Brief

We listen first

A call or a WhatsApp thread. What the business sells, who buys it, and what the site has to make happen. Free, and you leave with a plan whether you hire us or not.

02 / Quote

A fixed price, in writing

Scope, timeline and total, sent as a document. No hourly surprises. Half to start, half on handover.

03 / Build

You watch it happen

A live preview link from day two. You comment, we adjust — the design is settled before a single page is finished.

04 / Handover

It becomes yours

Files, domain, hosting and logins in your name, plus a walkthrough recording. Thirty days of fixes at no charge.
