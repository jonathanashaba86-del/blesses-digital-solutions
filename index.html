Questions

Before you call us

How long does a shop website take?
About four weeks from the day we agree the brief, assuming we get your product list and photos in the first week. A simpler business website is about two weeks. The thing that slows projects down is almost always waiting on content, so we help you gather it.
Can customers pay with MTN MoMo and Airtel Money?
Yes, and most do. We connect your own merchant account so money lands in your account, not ours. Card payments and cash on delivery work alongside it. The customer picks at checkout and the site handles each one differently.
Who owns the website when it is finished?
You do — completely. The domain is registered in your name, the code is yours, and we hand over every account and password. If you ever want another developer to take over, they can, and we will help them get started.
Can our own staff update products and prices?
That is the point of the back office. Adding a product, changing a price, marking something out of stock or processing an order are all done by your staff without touching code. We train your team before going live and stay on call afterwards.
What if we already have a website?
We will look at it honestly and tell you whether it is worth improving or worth replacing. Sometimes the answer is that you need better photos and a working contact form, not a new site. We would rather tell you that than sell you something.
What does it cost to keep running?
Hosting, domain and email for a shop site run about 600,000 UGX a year. The first year is included in the build price. Support beyond the included period is either pay-as-you-need or the monthly Growth plan.
Do you work with businesses outside Kampala?
Yes. Most of the work happens over calls and WhatsApp anyway. For shops we prefer to visit once at the start to see how the place actually runs — that visit is worth more than a week of emails.
