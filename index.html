The method

Four rotations
from browse to box.

Rotation 01

Spin before you decide

A flat photo hides the seam, the drape, the back panel. Drag any piece through 360° and judge it the way you would in a fitting room.

Rotation 02

Colour changes on the garment

Swatches don't sit in a corner here. Pick a colourway and the piece itself recolours while it turns, so you see the real thing.

Rotation 03

Stock you can count

Every size shows the exact number left. Nothing goes in your bag that we can't put in a box the same week.

Rotation 04

Checkout on WhatsApp

Your bag becomes a message. Confirm on the app you already use, pay on mobile money or card, and we dispatch from Kampala.

The atelier

Cut here.
Worn everywhere.

MANNIE works from a studio in Kampala with tailors, leatherworkers and print houses across the region — alongside pieces sourced from partners in Milan, Lagos and Seoul.

Kitenge & Ankara

Regional print, cut sharp

Wax prints sourced from regional mills and tailored into modern silhouettes — not costume, just very good clothes.

Leather

Belts, bags, boots

Vegetable-tanned hides finished by hand. Edges burnished, hardware solid brass, stitching done to last a decade.

Knitwear

Weight you can feel

Nothing under 240gsm. Every knit is specified by gram weight, so you know what arrives before it does.

Fit

Graded on real bodies

Sizes XS through 3XL on most cuts, graded on fit models rather than scaled from a single sample.
