Questions

Frequently asked.

Are these four products available today?

They are concept and design briefs from the BDS Product Lab, released in build order. VoiceCore ships first because it rides infrastructure we already run; ScoreCore, RadarCore and EmployCore open as new lines behind it. Founding clients are onboarded during the build, not after.

Why does BDS sell before it builds?

Because a product nobody has paid for is a hobby. We prove demand with a small number of founding clients, build against their real workflow, and only then open the line. It is the single reason our products ship finished rather than theoretical.

What does a founding client get?

Direct influence over the roadmap, a persona and reporting designed around their business, and pricing held for the length of the founding term. In exchange we ask for honest weekly feedback during the build.

How is pricing structured?

Never as a software licence. Each product is priced against the business outcome it protects — one missed customer, one bad loan, one invisible listing, one role you cannot yet afford to hire. Exact figures are quoted after a briefing call, because they depend on volume and scope.

Is our data safe?

Sensitive integrations are read-only by default, nothing acts without approval until you switch it on, and every product keeps a full audit trail. ScoreCore in particular is framed under ComplyCore UG so that data handling stands up to UMRA and internal compliance review.

Do you work with businesses outside Uganda?

Yes for web, brand and marketing work. The four Product Lab lines are tuned to Ugandan financial and communication behaviour first — MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, WhatsApp-led enquiries, English, Luganda and Swahili — and travel to comparable markets from there.
